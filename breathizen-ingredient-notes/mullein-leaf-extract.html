Mullein Leaf Extract 10:1

On the BreathiZen label: named first, concentrated ten to one.

About Mullein Leaf Extract 10:1

In short: Named first, concentrated ten to one.*

Mullein (Verbascum thapsus) is a tall, woolly-leaved plant that herbal traditions have used for the throat and chest for a very long time. BreathiZen names its leaf extract first in the 154 mg blend, and the 10:1 means about ten parts of leaf were concentrated into one.

See the ingredients page and safety and interactions.

Where It Grows

A tall plant with soft, wide leaves found across Europe, Asia and North America. See what is mullein and how is it used.

What 10:1 Means

Roughly ten parts of leaf make one part of concentrate. See what a 10:1 extract ratio means.

What Research Shows

Human trials are scarce, so it is a traditional botanical and not a proven lung treatment. See the benefits.

Who Should Ask First

Human research on mullein is limited and mostly traditional, so treat it as background and not as proof. Ask a doctor first if you take medicine, are pregnant or nursing, or live with a lung condition. See safety and interactions.
