How BreathiZen Works and How To Use It: Four Sprays Once a Day

What the spray is, how it is taken, and what to expect.

What the Spray Does

BreathiZen is a supplement and not a drug. It delivers three named plant extracts in a liquid by spray into the mouth, and the seller positions it to support clear breathing and healthy lung function as part of a healthy lifestyle.* See the ingredients and the benefits.

Four Steps

Follow these with the morning routine beside you.

1

Read the label

Study the directions, caution and Supplement Facts on your bottle.

2

Shake well

Shake before each use so the extracts are evenly mixed.

3

Spray four times

One serving is four pumps (0.60 mL) into the mouth or onto the tongue, once a day.

4

Pick the morning

The seller suggests the morning, ideally before the first meal.

Tips

Small habits make it stick. See breathing wellness daily habits.

  • Consistency matters more than the exact time.
  • Keep the bottle beside something you use daily.
  • Sip water afterwards if you like, since lemon is acidic.
  • Store as the label directs, and keep it out of reach of children.

How Long Does a Bottle Last?

A 60 mL bottle holds about 100 servings, so roughly 100 days. See pricing.
