What is GLPro Patches, Exactly?

GLPro Patches belongs to the category of transdermal wellness products — patches that deliver their formula through the skin rather than through digestion. The brand positions it around supporting blood sugar already within a normal range, appetite and craving control, and natural GLP-1 production support. It's marketed toward adults curious about an alternative to capsules and powders for everyday metabolic wellness, not as a medical treatment. The product's target audience is adults 18 and older who want a simple, once-daily routine without swallowing another pill.
